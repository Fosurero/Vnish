Vnishhub monitoring

ASIC Mining Monitoring Platform — Control, Automate & Optimize your Miners (support Bitmain and Whatsminer models with stock firmware)

VNISHHUB is a remote monitoring and management system for ASIC miners. The platform enables real-time tracking of hashrate, temperature, power consumption, and hardware status, while providing instant notifications and allowing operators to respond quickly to failures. VNISHHUB Monitoring is suitable for both individual miners and large-scale mining farms, improving operational stability, transparency, and efficiency.
